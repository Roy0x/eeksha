01 — The problem

Field robotics has a data readiness problem.

Field observations vary across crops, sites and seasons. Perception engineers must prepare aligned sensor inputs, review semantic labels and verify geometry before training and evaluating robots.

SEASONAL

Narrow field windows

Crop growth, weather and field access limit the time available to collect representative data.

COMPLEX

Occlusion & variation

Overlapping plants, lighting and missing depth make sensor datasets difficult to annotate and review.

FRAGMENTED

Repeated handoffs

Calibration, labeling, reconstruction and QA often live in separate workflows.

02 — Working MVP

One inspectable path from RGB-D to semantic 3D.

Eeksha currently processes existing, aligned RGB and depth inputs with camera calibration. It is a local research application, not a deployed multi-sensor capture or SaaS platform.

STEP 01

Import

Read RGB, depth and verified camera calibration from matching observations.

STEP 02

Label

Draw semantic polygons and review or correct masks.

STEP 03

Reconstruct

Create a calibrated semantic point cloud from the aligned sample.

STEP 04

QA & export

Inspect coverage, hashes and structured export artifacts including PLY, NPZ and JSON.
